## KQ1. Style cards: Indian travel / landscape / photography creators (who they are, what they do, what is replicable on a phone)

### Takeaway
The Indian travel-creator field in 2026 splits into (a) mega "global adventure/luxury" accounts (2–5M, English-first, high production), (b) the Hinglish/Hindi "budget + logistics" vloggers who built on YouTube and port cut-downs to Reels (Mountain Trekker, Nomadic Indian, Travelling Desi, Visa2Explore), (c) poetic/photographic storytellers (@abhiandnow, Anunay Sood's lineage, Kashmir cinematographers), and (d) a deep bench of Maharashtra-local Sahyadri/Pune micro-creators (12K–150K) whose "works 9-5, travels on weekends" persona is the closest match to an office-trip Khandala post. The phone-replicable techniques that recur across sources are: trending/retro Bollywood audio, Hinglish on-screen text, cost/itinerary breakdowns, lo-fi phone footage over studio polish, and poetry/quote captions.

### Cited Findings

#### Market context
- India has 413.85 million Reels users (described as the world's largest market); Hinglish (Hindi-English mix) outperforms single languages for educational Reels; Bollywood trending audio "cycles fast (days after film release)"; best India posting windows 7–9 AM and 8–11 PM IST, Sunday peak; minimum 3 Reels/week for growth — [Vaizle, Instagram Reel Trends (updated 31 Jul 2026)](https://insights.vaizle.com/instagram-reel-trends/)
- "15 of the top 20 most-used songs on Instagram reels globally came from Indian artists" (2022 data); retro audio (e.g. "Tumsa Koi Pyaara") and original sounds like "Kacha Badam" charted alongside "Srivalli", "Kesariya", "Baarish Main Tum" — [Meta Reels in Review India 2022](https://about.fb.com/news/2022/12/reels-in-review-some-of-2022s-top-trends-on-reels-in-india/) (older data, flagged)
- Instagram is reviving old Bollywood songs ("In The Night No Control" 1996, "Namak Ishq Ka" 2006, "Sajna Hai Mujhe" 1973) through repetition/discovery mechanics: "Once an audio starts appearing repeatedly in Reels, it can become familiar through repetition"; tapping an audio surfaces every Reel using it, creating a chain effect — [Brut India, 10 May 2026](https://www.brut.media/in/articles/from-in-the-night-no-control-to-sajna-hai-mujhe-how-instagram-is-bringing-old-bollywood-songs-back)
- Lo-fi authentic content: "50% of Instagram users prefer humorous, unpolished Reels"; Delhi-based food creators and D2C brands "consistently outperform studio-shot content using raw phone-camera Reels, with the rawness reading as authenticity to Indian audiences" — [Vaizle](https://insights.vaizle.com/instagram-reel-trends/)
- Ranking signals 2026: watch time/completion first, saves (reusable value), reshares and DM sends (strongest virality), trending audio (secondary discovery), replays — [Vaizle](https://insights.vaizle.com/instagram-reel-trends/)
- Carousels in 2026 "consistently outperform single-image posts and most Reels in terms of reach, saves, and shares"; Trial Reels let creators test content with non-followers first — [PostEverywhere, Instagram Trends 2026](https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/instagram-trends-2026)
- Instagram Reel rate card India 2026 (for context on who is "pro"): nano 1K–10K Rs 2,000–10,000 per Reel; micro 10K–100K Rs 8,000–75,000; mid 100K–500K Rs 50,000–3.5L; macro 500K–1M Rs 2L–8.5L — [TickTime Media, Influencer Rate Card India 2026](https://ticktime.media/blogs/influencer-rate-card-india-2026-instagram-reel-story-youtube-video-pricing)

## KQ3. Khandala/Lonavala specifics: sunset viewpoints, October light and conditions, hashtags/keywords, how existing reels are built, local SEO
