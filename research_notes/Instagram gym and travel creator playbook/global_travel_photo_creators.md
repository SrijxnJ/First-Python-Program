Research date: 7 Oct 2026. Instagram itself is login-walled, so follower counts come from third-party trackers and press; the date of each number is flagged. Anything marked **(inferred)** is my synthesis from the cited sources rather than a directly sourced fact. Many "exact slider value" pages (imagen-ai, medialoot, tutsplus, bwillcreative, hypeauditor profile pages) returned HTTP 403 to the fetcher; where values below come from search-result snippets of those pages, the URL is still cited but the full page could not be read.

## KQ1. Who are the most famous and fastest-rising global travel/landscape/photography creators, and what are their style cards?

### Takeaway
The "mega" tier in 2026 is still dominated by legacy names (Beautiful Destinations ~24M, Jordi Koalitic 7.3M IG / 20.6M TikTok, Paul Nicklen 7M+, Chris Burkard ~3–3.8M, Murad Osmann ~3.1M, Peter McKinnon 3M+, Jack Morris / Lauren Bullen ~2M+ each), but the growth engine has shifted to Reels/TikTok-native creators whose formats are information-led (cost, mistakes, POV, "nobody tells you") rather than purely aesthetic — e.g. Drew Binsky (~700K IG vs ~3.9M TikTok), Alex Ojeda (660K vs 3.9M), Salah Brooks (316K vs 3.9M), and reel-view leaders like @adamboro, @helenowen, @angeldei_ and @fuelyourwander. Of the names the brief asked me to verify, Hayden Cahill and Kyle Hjelmeseth could not be verified as travel photographers, and "Hoang Le" most plausibly maps to Vietnamese adventure traveller Hoang Le Giang.

## KQ2. Which travel formats got the most shares/saves in 2025–2026, and what are the main travel reel editing styles?

### Takeaway
Information-led formats win saves and shares: exact cost breakdowns, "things nobody tells you"/mistake hooks, comparison ("is X worth it?"), photo slideshows (camera-roll dumps) with trending audio, and three-panel "what I wore / where I went / what I did" splits; Reels drive comments and shares while carousels drive saves, and Reels shares rose ~67% YoY per Hootsuite's 2026 report. Editing-wise, 2026 short-form splits into "documentary realism" (slow, 24fps feel, single grade) and "phone-first cinematic" (tripod, manual exposure, one LUT) at the premium end, versus lo-fi/raw phone footage at the authentic end.

### Inferences
- A practical 2026 travel-reel stack for an iPhone creator: (1) hook text naming the place within 2 s; (2) 20–45 s; (3) 3–5 s cuts for fast formats, longer holds for "documentary realism"; (4) one grade/LUT across all clips; (5) end with a save-trigger (cost table, itinerary, "mistake to avoid").
- Drone-free alternatives that mimic aerial energy: 0.5x hyperlapse walking toward a landmark, top-down shots from balconies/bridges, tripod time-lapse of clouds/crowds, slow 0.5x pans on a mini tripod head, and "stomp-to-reveal" match cuts (inferred from the Vaizle/Adventure Dispatch/Splice sources).
- The "camera-roll dump" reel is essentially the Vaizle "photo slideshow with trending audio" format, which is the cheapest high-save format available.

## KQ3. Colour grades (golden-hour warm, teal-orange, moody film, pastel, Fuji/Kodak emulation) with Lightroom Mobile / CapCut / VN slider values

### Takeaway
Only the golden-hour look has a fully numeric public recipe (Imagen's: Temp +12, Tint +4, Highlights -30, Shadows +35, Vibrance +15, Orange lum +12, Yellow hue -10); teal-orange, moody, pastel and film looks are documented directionally (which slider, which direction) with just a few hard numbers (e.g. Calibration Blue Primary Hue -100 / Red Primary Hue +50 for teal-orange; split-tone balance ~75/25 for duotone moody). No source gives CapCut or VN numeric values; those apps rely on LUT import.

### Cited Findings
**Golden-hour warm (Lightroom Mobile)**
- Imagen's golden-hour recipe: Exposure +0.30, Contrast +10, Highlights -30, Shadows +35, Whites +5, Blacks -7; Temperature +12, Tint +4 ("gentle rose undertone"), Vibrance +15, Saturation +5; HSL Orange: Hue -5 / Sat -10 / Lum +12; Yellow: Hue -10 / Sat +10 / Lum +10 — [Imagen AI golden-hour preset](https://imagen-ai.com/post/golden-hour-lightroom-preset/) (page returned 403 on fetch; values are from the search snippet of this page).
- "Slide the White Balance up to around 6800K for a warm evening feel" — [Light Stalking](https://www.lightstalking.com/boost-golden-hour-images-lightroom/).
- Set WB to Daylight, "nudge the temperature to the warmer side and add a slight magenta tint"; HSL: slide Yellow toward Orange, lift Orange Luminance for skin, pull Blue Luminance down to deepen the sky; lower exposure to protect sky, lift shadows, "modest contrast boost"; sharpen radius 0.6–1.0 px — [The Social Cat](https://thesocialcat.com/blog/how-to-edit-your-photos-to-enhance-golden-hour-glow).
- Golden-hour light itself is ~3,000 K — [Nature TTL](https://naturettl.com/editing-guide-golden-hour-photography).

**Teal & orange**
- Calibration: Blue Primary Hue -100, Red Primary Hue +50, then reduce saturation; raise the bottom-left tone-curve point "a bit" for lifted blacks; slightly increase Texture, Clarity, Dehaze — [ExpertPhotography](https://expertphotography.com/teal-and-orange-lightroom).
- HSL: move Blue hue left (toward aqua/teal) and Aqua right; Color Grading: orange in highlights, blue/teal in shadows — [Digital Photography School](https://digital-photography-school.com/lightroom-color-grading); [Shotkit teal-orange](https://shotkit.com/teal-orange-look/).
- Tuts+ route: Color Mix → shift Yellow hue toward orange, raise Orange saturation; Grading → (as summarised) shadows warmed, highlights pushed to cyan — [Tuts+](https://photography.tutsplus.com/tutorials/how-to-create-the-orange-and-teal-look-in-photos-with-lightroom-classic-diy-style-preset--cms-36646) (page 403'd; summary from snippet, and note it is the reverse of the more common orange-highlights/teal-shadows split in the DPS guide).
- Preset vendors describe the look as "blues become teal, oranges more orange, yellows richer/warmer", often with teal pushed into shadows and overall exposure lowered for mood — [SparkleStock](https://www.sparklestock.com/downloads/teal-orange-presets/).
- "There are no set values to use" — [ExpertPhotography](https://expertphotography.com/teal-and-orange-lightroom).

**Moody / film**
- Split toning in Lightroom Mobile (under the Effects/aperture icon): e.g. blue in highlights, green in shadows, Balance set so one colour dominates (~75% blue / 25% green); keep it subtle — [Lightroom Killer Tips](https://lightroomkillertips.com/creating-beautiful-duotones-right-within-lightroom-mobile).
- Add grain "conservatively" as the final touch — [Imaginated moody portraits](https://www.imaginated.com/blog/how-to-edit-moody-portraits-in-lightroom).
- Lightroom's built-in "Film-Inspired" presets can be applied then tweaked in Light/Color/Grain on mobile — [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/africa/learn/lightroom-cc/web/create-film-look-lightroom-mobile-presets).
- PetaPixel's six moody-landscape tips exist but the page 403'd — [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2018/11/29/6-lightroom-tips-to-create-moody-landscape-photos/) (dated 2018).

**Kodak Portra / Fuji emulation**
- Portra 400 look = "soft contrast, creamy skin tones, muted color saturation, and a gentle lift in the black point"; use the Point Curve: drag the bottom-left point up slightly (no true black), build a "gentle, wide S" rather than a steep one, keep the top-right point anchored for a slow highlight roll-off; then HSL + Split Toning — [Imagen AI Portra guide](https://imagen-ai.com/post/lightroom-portra-400-preset/) (snippet; page 403'd).
- Portra characteristics to emulate: reds/oranges "slightly lifted but never pushed into orange-pumpkin"; shadows "lift to a warm dark grey, sometimes with a slight blue or magenta undertone"; highlights roll smoothly into white; fine irregular grain; Portra's warm bias gives "too much orange for darker skin" — Fuji-style presets recommended for darker and East-Asian skin — [Mastin Labs](https://mastinlabs.com/blogs/photoism/blog-portra-400-lightroom-authentic-look).
- Free Portra/Kodak preset round-ups exist ("Summer Portra", "Kodak Crisp") — [Gridfiti](https://gridfiti.com/film-emulation-presets).

**Pastel**
- Lift Shadows and Blacks to soften the darkest areas, darken highlights (flatter "HDR-ish" contrast), warm the white balance, desaturate some colours, then add pastel colour grading — [BWillCreative pastel (mobile)](https://www.bwillcreative.com/?p=69220); [Medialoot pastel](https://medialoot.com/blog/make-a-pastel-effect-in-lightroom) (Medialoot 403'd; directional only).

**Video apps**
- CapCut: HSL, curves, color wheels (shadows/midtones/highlights), LUTs; vibrance preferred over saturation — [CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/resource/cinematic-color-grading). No numeric travel recipe found.
- VN: .cube LUT import via Creation Kit → Filters; cinematic LUT packs for Rec.709 phone footage exist (e.g. "Explore Cinematic LUTs") — [Filmora/VN](https://filmora.wondershare.com.ru/video-editing/vn-editor-adding-luts.html); [Josh Falls LUTs](https://joshfalls.gumroad.com/l/explorecinematicluts).

## KQ6. What makes a group/friends/office trip reel shareable?

### Takeaway
Shareability in 2026 is driven by emotional resonance plus practical value, relatability that makes someone tag a friend, a visual hook in the first 3 seconds, sub-30-second length, and a clear "send this to your travel buddy" CTA; for group trips, collaborative capture (everyone contributes clips), candid moments and recognisable formats (recap montage with one song, POV "you're on the trip", "what I wore/where I went/what I did") convert.

### Cited Findings
- Most shareable formats: Reels under 30 s with a visual hook in the first 3 s; carousels for storytelling; UGC; "clear calls-to-action asking followers to share with friends"; the algorithm prioritises "shares, saves, and watch time" over likes; "emotional resonance and practical value" — [Sked Social shareability](https://skedsocial.com/blog/how-to-increase-your-instagram-post-shareability-in-2026.md).

### Inferences

- Candid group frames that read as "real" (walking-and-talking from behind, laughing at a table, the jump shot on 3) outperform posed line-ups because 2026 guidance consistently rewards authenticity/UGC over polish.
- Collaborative capture (each person shoots 3–5 vertical clips on their phone, pooled via AirDrop/shared album) is the phone-era equivalent of a multi-cam shoot and is what the group-template tools are built around.
