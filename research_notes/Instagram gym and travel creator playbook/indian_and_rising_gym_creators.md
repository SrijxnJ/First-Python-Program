# Indian and Rising Gym/Fitness Creators on Instagram: Style Cards and Formulas

Research window: searched and fetched 6–7 Oct 2026. Instagram itself cannot be read without login, so follower counts come from influencer-agency databases (Modash, Qoruz, Favikon, Famekeeda, ViralPitch, HypeAuditor, SocialVeins) and Indian press. These databases disagree with each other by 10–30% and snapshot at different dates (noted per card). Style details that are NOT directly sourced (camera angle, colour grade, edit pace, music) are labelled **[inferred]** and should be verified by Srijan watching the account for 10 minutes before copying.

Reading guide for the style cards: each card has (a) sourced facts with links, (b) an `[inferred]` block derived from how sources describe the creator plus the general format conventions documented in KQ3/KQ4. Do not treat the `[inferred]` lines as verified fact.

## KQ1: Indian fitness creators and their formulas (big names and established mid-tier)

### Takeaway
The Indian fitness creator economy splits into four formulas: (1) **desi-roots authenticity** (Ankit Baiyanpuria, Rahul Dhandlaniya, Sunder Sangwan: Haryanvi greetings, akhara drills, handheld phone footage, village backdrops, milk/ghee diet); (2) **science/coach explainers** (Rohit Khatri, Guru Mann, Biglee Murali, Abhinav Mahajan: English/Hinglish talk-to-camera, step-by-step routines, myth-busting); (3) **physique/aspiration** (Sahil Khan, Pawan Sahu, Mukesh Gahlot, Jeet Selal, Bhuwan Chauhan: competition bodies, luxury lifestyle, supplement sponsorships); and (4) **humour/relatability** (Mihir Lifts, Dr. Destruction's aggressive persona, Vidhi Dahad's comedy gym reels). For an office-going solo filmer with an iPhone, formulas 1 and 4 are the replicable ones; 2 requires credentials to be credible; 3 requires a competition physique.

### Inferences
- The two most-copied Indian formulas of 2023–2026 are the daily numbered challenge (Baiyanpuria) and humour-wrapped lifting (Mihir Lifts). Both are physique-agnostic and phone-shot, which fits Srijan.
- Almost every mid-tier Indian account listed is positioned by a one-line niche ("skip", "military calisthenics", "natural aesthetics", "memes + lifts"). Accounts described only as "fitness" sit lower in the lists. A corporate-employee framing ("9-to-5 + gym in Pune") is an unoccupied one-liner in every list reviewed.
- Agency lists heavily feature coaches with certifications, which suggests the explainer formula depends on credibility signals Srijan does not have yet; the vlog/challenge/humour lanes do not.
- Language: most lists tag creators as "English" but the biggest growth stories (Baiyanpuria, Dhandlaniya, Sangwan) are Haryanvi/Hindi, and the mid-tier is Hinglish. English-only skews to Pilates/yoga/coach niches.
