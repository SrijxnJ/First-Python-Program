# Srijan's Social Media Brain

One place for everything we learn about making content in any genre. Each video, reel or tutorial we study gets a lesson file in `lessons/`. The useful techniques from it are then filed under the topics below, so this page becomes the cheat sheet.

Rules for adding to the Brain:
- Record what the source actually shows or says. Mark our own interpretation as *(our take)*.
- Every technique says **when to use it** and **how to do it** in Edits / CapCut / VN.
- Link each technique back to its lesson file.

Tools, repos and skills we find along the way go to [PARKED.md](PARKED.md) and get reviewed before anything is installed.

## Topics
1. **Content & hooks**: ideas, formats, the first 3 seconds, storytelling, CTAs
2. **Editing & pacing**: cuts, speed ramps, rhythm, length
3. **Sound**: music choice, beat sync, SFX, voice, mixing levels
4. **Effects**: zooms, shakes, flashes, blur, overlays
5. **Transitions**: when to use them, how to build them, when to skip them
6. **Text & fonts**: placement, safe zones, animation, font pairing
7. **Colour**: grades by genre, slider recipes
8. **Filming**: angles, lighting, phone settings, tripod
9. **Growth**: algorithm, posting, captions, Trial Reels, analytics

## Cheat sheet (grows with every lesson)
**Content & hooks**
- First words = context, delivered by audio + visuals + on-screen text together. Delaying it raises skip rate. [L01]
- Then *lean*: proof/stat, a pain point or benefit, or something shocking. [L01]
- Then *snapback*: pull the viewer opposite to what they expect. [L01]

**Content & hooks (cont.)**
- Contrast hook: "Looks X. Sounds/feels like Y." [L02]
- An honest limitation ("the honest part…") makes the rest believable. [L02]

**Editing & pacing**
- Change something on screen every 1.5–2.5 s (cut, layout swap, graphic). [L01]

**Sound**
- One quiet music bed plus an SFX for every visual event: whoosh = move, pop = text, chime = success, thud = impact. [L01]

**Sound (cont.)**
- No SFX = "silent movie". Free CC0 sounds come from Openverse. AI-assisted placement and critique: Real SFX skill + earshot (both parked, pending install). [L02]

**Effects**
- Number counters (e.g. counting up to 75 KG) and n/3 progress badges for list videos. [L02]

**Text & fonts**
- Teaching content: clean sans, white/yellow on dark, red = wrong, green = right. [L01]
- Emphasis words in serif italic, body in clean sans, keyword highlights in yellow/orange. [L02]

**Growth**
- CTA: "Comment *keyword* and I'll send it to you", with social proof of what they'll get. [L01]
